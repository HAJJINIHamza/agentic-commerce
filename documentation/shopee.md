### What should I know as a store in shopee 
- KOTRA (Korea Trade-Investment Promotion Agency) is a South Korean government agency that helps Korean companies export abroad, find overseas buyers, and expand into international markets.

- If you're selling internationally, KOTRA can help reduce your logistics costs and improve delivery speed.

- ELigibility for KOTRA services : 
    You must be a Korean SME or mid-sized company (중소·중견기업).
    You must have:
    export sales history, or
    an export contract,
    depending on the specific program.

- KOTRA will pay back part of my marketing costs
If you:
Attend the required Shopee/KOTRA training,
Open your Shopee store,
List products,

then KOTRA may reimburse 70% of eligible marketing expenses, up to ₩300,000.

- You can claim expenses from KOTRA for : Shopee Ads, product content creation, mandatory certifications, SNS ads, external advertising.

- ASEAN (Association of southeast asian nations) is a huge opportunity

    ASEAN is a successful regional alliance of 10 countries with 667M people and a $3.3T economy.
    ASEAN has ~670 million consumers.
    The population is young.
    The middle class is growing.
    E-commerce adoption is high.
    K-Beauty is extremely popular.
