### Marketing basic rules

11.2 Simple Rules
High impressions + low clicks
→ title or image is weak

High clicks + low orders
→ price or product page is weak

Many orders + low profit
→ pricing is wrong

Good profit + good reviews
→ scale product

Bad reviews
→ stop or change supplier

### Shopee Ads

#### Allows us to : 


1. Promote My Products

    Promote products to shoppers actively searching with related keywords or viewing similar products. 

    Increased Exposure: You can show ads to highly relevant customers, such as at the top of the search results page.
    Increased Sales: You can expand your reach to potential customers searching for product-related keywords.

Show your shop on search result page to attract more buyers. 

4. Promote Live
Promote your Shopee Live room to interested shoppers to drive traffic.

#### Advertising fee billing method
Advertising costs are incurred only when a consumer clicks on an ad. Advertising costs are deducted from advertising credits.

Shopee automatically determines the validity of clicks and does not charge sellers for invalid clicks. If the same user clicks repeatedly, or if the click is generated by an automated bot, it is considered an invalid click.
