⭐️ TOP HIGHLIGHTS — Before You Buy

1) 35ml Flood Hydration

Each sheet is infused with 35ml essence for a rich, comforting hydration feel.

Use the remaining essence on neck and body areas for extended moisture care.

2) CICA + Multi-Peptide Balance Care

A focused blend of CICA, peptides, Panthenol, and Hyaluronic Acid helps calm stressed-looking skin and support a smoother, firmer-looking finish.

3) Bemliese™ Skin-Hugging Sheet

Made with a cotton-linter-based Bemliese™ sheet for a soft, close fit that helps keep essence in contact with skin.

*to the stratum corneum

4) Fresh, Non-Sticky Finish for SG Climate

Designed for humid weather and indoor A/C environments: deep moisture feel with a fresh, non-greasy finish.

🌊 WHY AGESSENCE QUIET GLOW CICA SHEET MASK?

Daily skin is constantly challenged by heat, humidity, and dryness from air-conditioned spaces.

This mask is designed to deliver Flood-to-Firm comfort: intensive hydration, calming care, and a refined Quiet Glow look in one step.

With a skin-hugging sheet and high-volume essence delivery, it supports:

• better moisture comfort

• smoother-looking texture

• balanced-looking skin condition

• a soft glow without heaviness

───

💡 HOW TO USE

Step 1 — Prep

After cleansing and toner, apply the mask evenly to face.

Step 2 — Relax (15–20 min)

Leave on for 15–20 minutes.

Do not wait until the sheet becomes fully dry.

Step 3 — Finish

Remove the mask and gently pat in remaining essence.

Pro Tip

Apply extra essence from the pouch to neck, elbows, and knees for all-over moisture care.
